Privacy Policy for Auto Parts India

Last updated: August 2026

Auto Parts India ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Auto Parts India mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Name and profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account authentication information
  • Automobile spare-part listing information
  • Photos uploaded to listings
  • Location information when you use location features
  • Messages exchanged through the in-app chat
  • Device and app information required for security and functionality

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide buying and selling marketplace services
  • Display spare-part listings
  • Provide location-based features
  • Enable communication between buyers and sellers
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Send important notifications related to the application
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity

3. Location Information

Auto Parts India may request access to your device location when you use location-based features. Location information may be used to show nearby listings, determine your selected location, and improve location-based search functionality.

You can control location permissions through your Android device settings.

4. Photos and User Content

When you create a listing, you may upload photos and other information about automobile spare parts. This information may be displayed to other users as part of the marketplace listing.

5. Authentication

We may use authentication services such as Firebase Authentication to securely create and manage user accounts.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.
